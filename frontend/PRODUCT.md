# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + Vanilla CSS

## Users

University students seeking campus event discovery, peer networking, and student group collaboration.

## Product Purpose

Provide a unified, engaging digital hub for campus life—enabling students to discover events, connect with peers, and participate in campus organizations seamlessly.

## Positioning

A hyper-local campus engagement platform built specifically for university student ecosystems, distinct from generic social networks or enterprise productivity tools.

## Operating Context

Used on desktop and mobile web browsers during everyday campus routines—between classes, planning weekend events, or exploring campus clubs and student activities.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Event discovery, scheduling, and categorical filtering (academic, social, sports, clubs).
- Student peer discovery and community networking.
- Student group and organization directory.
- Web platform built with React, Vite, and Vanilla CSS.

## Brand Commitments

- Institution: SIES Graduate School of Technology (SIES GST).
- Closed-campus ecosystem exclusively for SIES GST students, faculty, and student organizations.

## Evidence on Hand

- No initial media assets or live data provided yet.

## Product Principles

1. **Frictionless Discovery**: Make finding events and clubs effortless within seconds.
2. **Community First**: Foster genuine student-to-student connection and group participation.
3. **Dynamic & Alive**: The interface should feel vibrant, active, and relevant to modern student life.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA standard for color contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader accessibility, and mobile touch targets.
